App name
GARON DENISE

Short description
Track leads, purchase requests, supply orders, and containers in one local operations workspace.

Full description
GARON DENISE helps business teams keep daily operations moving without switching between paper notes and scattered spreadsheets. The app organizes lead follow-ups, procurement requests, supply orders, and container movements in a focused Android workspace.

Create records with owners, priorities, due dates, values, locations, progress, and notes. Review active work from the dashboard, open category-specific lists, update statuses, and keep preference settings stored locally on the device.

The app works without accounts, cloud sync, analytics, ads, or external service connections.
